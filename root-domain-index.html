⚙️ Architecture & Kinematic Implementation

The VEX V5 LemLib Suite provides competition teams with high-precision autonomous motion profiling. Leveraging LemLib's pure pursuit algorithms and dual-wheel odometry tracking, teams model deterministic 15-second autonomous win-point routines and 60-second programming skills runs on the Override game field.

Built for teams authoring PROS C++, this suite combines visual waypoint sequencing with direct source code synchronization, USB V5 Brain telemetry streaming, and cross-file variable indexing.

👤 About Us & Author Qualifications

Who We Are: The VEX V5 LemLib Suite was architected and developed by dedicated VEX Robotics coders and autonomous systems engineers with years of first-hand competition experience in the VEX Robotics Competition (VRC).

Background & Expertise: Having competed across multiple VRC seasons (including Spin Up, Over Under, High Stakes, and Override), our engineering focus centers on high-reliability robot kinematics, discrete odometry integration loops ($10\,\text{ms}$ tracking cycles), non-linear Pure Pursuit algorithms, and Boomerang pursuit controllers. We specialize in authoring production-grade C++ code on the open-source PROS kernel and the LemLib motion library.

🏆 Engineering Pedagogy

We built this platform to eliminate guesswork in competitive autonomous routines. Traditional autonomous development often requires hundreds of trial-and-error battery cycles. This toolchain gives teams mathematical certainty through real-time kinematics simulation and AST code translation.

💻 Open-Source Commitment

Our software is designed openly for the international VEX robotics educational community, offering free trajectory planning, AST translation, hardware serial communication, and modular PROS project scaffolding to student teams worldwide.
